04Rules of play

Aggression, betrayal, propaganda and coordinated warfare are the game. The following are not:

  • Automation. No bots, scripts, macros, or automated clients that play for you.
  • Exploits. If you find a bug that produces resources, skips a cost, or breaks a limit, report it. Using it — or telling others to — is grounds for a ban and a rollback.
  • Attacks on the service. No scraping at volumes that degrade the game, no denial-of-service, no attempts to access accounts, data, or systems that are not yours.
  • Reverse-engineering the server. Reading the public pages is fine. Probing for vulnerabilities is not, unless we have invited you to.
  • Harassment. Threats, slurs, sexual content, doxxing, or targeting a player outside the game. In-game hostility is expected; real-world harassment ends your account.
  • Impersonation. Do not pose as staff, moderators, or another player.
  • Illegal content. Anything unlawful in any name, description, or message.

Deception inside the game — breaking a treaty, spying on an ally, faking a peace offer — is a legitimate strategy and is not a violation of these terms.
