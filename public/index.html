What is SOVRA

A whole international system, run by real people

SOVRA is a persistent multiplayer nation simulation game you play in a browser. You start with one nation and a small treasury. Everything after that — the cities you found, the industries you build, the industries you specialise in, the wars you start and the ones started on you — is decided by you and by everyone else sharing the same world. There are no bots to farm and no campaign to finish. The world state is whatever the players last left it as.

◈

Nation

Found cities, set land and infrastructure, manage population growth against disease, crime and age. Density is a choice with consequences — pack people in and they start dying.

⌗

Economy

Raw extraction feeds manufacturing feeds construction. Refined steel, aluminium and munitions gate what you can build. Commerce and food run on the same clock as everything else.

⇄

Market

An open player-driven exchange. No NPC seller, no fixed prices. When a war breaks out, munitions spike — and everyone can see it happening in the order book.

✶

War

Ground, air and naval fronts, action points, resistance, espionage and missiles. Wars run for days, not minutes. Every battle roll is stored, so any fight can be replayed and audited.

The difference

The ledger shows its work

Most nation games hand you one number — your income — and let you guess where it came from. SOVRA unfolds it. Every line of your treasury, your population, your production and your military upkeep can be expanded down to the individual building that caused it.

  • Per-building attribution. See exactly which farm, factory or barracks is earning or bleeding.
  • Resource runway. Days until you run dry, at the current burn rate, per resource.
  • Idle diagnosis. A building producing nothing tells you why — missing input, no workers, or policy penalty.
  • No hidden multipliers. Policies and projects state their cost as loudly as their benefit.

Under the hood

Systems that talk to each other

Nothing in SOVRA is decorative. Change one thing and the consequences propagate through every other subsystem on the next turn.

01 / CITIES

Cities & Buildings

Each city has its own land, infrastructure and building slots. Commerce, civil and military buildings cost refined materials on top of money — you cannot buy your way past an industry you never built.

02 / PEOPLE

Population

Growth, disease, crime and age are simulated per city. Overcrowd without hospitals and the death rate answers for you. The formula is printed on the page.

03 / POLICY

Policies

Eighteen policies across economic, social and military slots — one active per slot, five-day lock. Every single one costs you something. There are no free bonuses in SOVRA.

04 / PROJECTS

National Projects

Twenty-seven permanent national investments, from intelligence agencies to arms stockpiles. Expensive, irreversible, and each one narrows what kind of nation you can still become.

05 / WAR

Combat & Espionage

Three-roll battle resolution across ground, air and naval theatres, plus resistance, blockades, spy operations and missiles. Seeded rolls mean every result is reproducible.

06 / MATCHMAKING

Score & War Range

Score is a power rating and a matchmaking weight at once. You can be reached by someone bigger, never by someone overwhelmingly bigger — so every unit you build is also a decision about who is allowed to attack you.

Being built next: alliances and shared banks · treaties · espionage interface · missiles and nuclear weapons. The engine already models all four — they do not have a page yet, and we would rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

Coming from Politics & War?

Same depth. Different contract with the player.

SOVRA is built for people who like this genre and have opinions about it. Here is what we changed on purpose.

What frustrated us

  • Formulas hidden, learned only from community spreadsheets
  • Domestic policies that are pure upside with no trade-off
  • Income appears as one number with no breakdown
  • Interfaces that assume a desktop and a second monitor
  • New players farmed before they understand the systems

What SOVRA does

  • Every formula shown in-game, in an expandable ledger
  • Every policy has a stated cost — enforced by an automated test
  • Per-building attribution on income, upkeep and production
  • Built mobile-first; full game playable on a phone
  • Score-banded war ranges and clear onboarding before exposure

Questions

Frequently asked

Is SOVRA free to play?
Yes. SOVRA is free and runs entirely in your browser — no download, no launcher, no account purchase required to play the full game.
What kind of game is SOVRA?
A persistent browser-based nation simulation — sometimes called a PBBG. You run one nation in a shared world alongside every other player: building cities, managing an economy, trading on a player market and fighting wars. It is closest in spirit to games like Politics and War, Cyber Nations and NationStates, with a heavier simulation underneath.
How much time does it take?
Turns process on a fixed cycle rather than in real time, so a few focused check-ins a day is enough to play competitively. Wars run over days, which means you are never punished for sleeping.
How does disease work?
Disease rate rises with population density and pollution and falls with hospitals. It is computed per city each turn and the full equation, with your own city's inputs substituted in, is shown on your population page — so you can see exactly how much a hospital is worth before you build it.
Can I play SOVRA on mobile?
Yes. Every page is built for phones — wide data tables collapse into readable cards, and the whole game including markets and war is usable on a small screen.
Are there alliances?
Not yet. Alliances, shared banks and treaties are the next major system being built, and they are the part of this genre that turns a nation simulator into a political one. Right now SOVRA is a shared world you fight and trade in as an individual. We would rather say that plainly than list a feature that is not there.
Is there pay-to-win?
No. Nothing purchasable affects nation strength, combat outcomes or economy. The advantage in SOVRA comes from understanding the systems and from who you are allied with.
