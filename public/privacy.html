SOVRA is a free browser game. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you play, why we need it, and what control you have over it. We collect as little as the game can function on, and we do not sell your personal data.

01Who we are

SOVRA — The World Order ("SOVRA", "we", "us") is a multiplayer nation simulation game operated at playsovra.com. For the purposes of the UK/EU General Data Protection Regulation, the data controller is The Living Kingdom, reachable at playsovra@gmail.com.

02What information we collect

Information you give us

DataDetail
Email addressUsed to identify your account and to reset your password. Not shown to other players.
Username / nation namePublic. Visible to every other player in the game.
PasswordStored only as a bcrypt hash. We never store, see, or are able to recover your plaintext password.
In-game textNation descriptions, alliance names and descriptions, and any message you send in game. Treat anything you type in game as public.

Information collected automatically

DataDetail
IP addressRecorded on login. Used for security and to detect players operating multiple accounts, which is prohibited by the Terms of Service.
Browser & deviceUser agent string, screen size, and language, so pages render correctly and we can reproduce bugs.
Gameplay dataYour nation, cities, resources, trades, wars, battle results and administrative actions taken on your account. This is the game state itself.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, postal address, date of birth, phone number, government identifiers, or payment information. SOVRA takes no payments, so no card data ever reaches us.

03Why we collect it

  • To run the game. Your account and nation cannot exist without the data above.
  • To keep the game fair. IP and device data are used to detect multi-accounting, botting, and abuse. A game like this is only worth playing if the rules are enforced.
  • To keep the service secure. Logs let us investigate attacks, account takeovers, and outages.
  • To contact you. Password resets, security notices, and — only if you opt in — occasional updates about the game.

If you are in the UK or EEA, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (running your account), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security, keeping the game playable), and consent (non-essential cookies and any marketing email).

04Cookies and browser storage

TypePurpose
EssentialAn authentication token that keeps you logged in between page loads. Without it, the game cannot work. It is not used for tracking.

AdvertisingNone. No advertising or tracking cookies are set. See section 5.

You can clear these at any time through your browser settings. Clearing the essential token simply logs you out.

05Advertising

SOVRA currently shows no advertising. No advertising cookies are set, no advertising network receives any information about you, and no third party is given your data for marketing.

If that changes — the game is free and hosting is not — this page will be updated before any ad is served, and this section will name the network and explain how to opt out. Two commitments will hold regardless:

  • We will never pass your email address, password, or game account data to an advertiser.
  • Advertising will never be placed inside the market, war, or ledger interfaces.

06Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it with exactly two service providers, both of which are required to run the game:

ProviderRole
RenderApplication hosting. Processes requests and server logs.
NeonManaged PostgreSQL database. Stores accounts and game state.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to investigate a credible threat to the safety of a person or the service.

Publicly visible in game: your nation name, leader name, alliance, score, city count, war history and public rankings position. This is game data other players need in order to play against you. Your email address is never public.

07How long we keep it

  • Active accounts: kept while the account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: personal data (email, password hash, IP records) removed within 30 days. Game history that involves other players — war records, trades, alliance membership — may be retained in anonymised form, because deleting it would rewrite other people's game history.
  • Server logs: retained for up to 90 days.
  • Ban and enforcement records: retained as long as necessary to keep a banned player from returning.

08Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including profiling for advertising.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Email playsovra@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EEA and are unhappy with our response, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and you will never be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

09Children

SOVRA is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, or 16 if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below these ages. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10Security

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plaintext.
  • Sessions use signed tokens; administrative privileges are checked against the database on every request, so access can be revoked instantly.
  • Every administrative action on an account is logged with a reason to an append-only record.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a password you do not reuse anywhere else, and tell us immediately if you believe your account has been accessed by someone else.

11International transfers

SOVRA is played worldwide and our infrastructure providers operate data centres in multiple countries, including the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK or EEA, it is protected by the transfer mechanisms our providers rely on, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and the "last updated" date at the top will move. If a change materially affects your rights, we will announce it in game before it takes effect.
